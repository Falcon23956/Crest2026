CRESTVISTA WEBSITE – HOW TO ADD A LISTING

1. Put the plot photos/videos in a folder called "photos" next to index.html
   (name them simply, e.g. a1-1.jpg, a1-2.jpg, a1.mp4). Keep photos under 300 KB each.
2. Open listings.js in Notepad. Copy one plot block (from { to }) and paste it below
   the last one, with a comma between blocks.
3. Change the details. For photos write: images: ["photos/a1-1.jpg","photos/a1-2.jpg"]
4. For video, upload to YouTube as "Unlisted" and paste the link:
   videos: ["https://youtu.be/XXXXXXXXXXX"]
   (Short MP4 files also work: videos: ["photos/a1.mp4"], keep each under 20 MB.)
5. Delete the three SAMPLE blocks. Save, then upload the changed files to GitHub.
6. Change the WhatsApp number or phone numbers at the top of listings.js.

Each plot needs a different id (e.g. konza-a1, konza-a2).
Or just send me the details and photos and I will update it for you.
